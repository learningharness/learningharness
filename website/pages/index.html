01 — THE REASON

Learning shouldn’t be
another task on your list.

A separate tutor needs you to stop, switch context and know what to ask. In the middle of shipping, that moment is easy to skip. The work gets done. The chance to understand it passes.

LearningHarness brings that opportunity into the work you’re already doing. A dedicated learning agent notices what’s worth understanding and offers a question, a visual or a short explanation. You choose when to engage. Your coding agent keeps building.

02 — THE EXPERIENCE
01

A useful idea. Not another feed.

Selected work events and your learning goals shape the opportunity. A caching tradeoff, a concurrency bug, a design decision worth understanding.

02

Learn by doing something with it.

Predict an outcome. Explore a visual. Work through a question. The learning agent chooses a tool for the concept and your level.

03

Build on what you actually know.

Local memory keeps your goals, practice evidence and review history. Future sessions can start where you left off.

PRIVACY

There is no learning backend.

Selected context goes to your existing Codex connection. LearningHarness runs locally; memory stays on your device. Usage metadata sharing with PostHog is on by default and can be turned off in Learn.

Follow the data ↗
CONTROL

Visible usage. An off switch.

You already spend tokens to get the work done. Put some toward understanding it, too. Learning uses your existing model allowance, with visible usage and controls to pause or stop.
