# Third-party notices

LearningHarness-owned source is MIT licensed. Dependencies and reused components retain their own licenses and copyright notices; the root MIT license does not replace them.

`package-lock.json` records the resolved dependency versions and license metadata. Review the installed packages' license files for their complete terms. Distribution packaging copies the notices for bundled SolidJS, parse5, entities and d3-hierarchy into `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. Build tooling and optional model integrations have their own dependency licenses.

Do not remove an existing attribution, copy source of uncertain provenance, or claim that a dependency is covered by the LearningHarness license. A release must include the notices for everything it actually bundles. The source cleanup is not a complete third-party legal audit.
