# Community conduct

Be considerate, specific and willing to learn. Discuss the idea or code rather than the person's worth or experience. Beginners, experienced developers and people using AI tools are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention, doxxing and publishing someone else's private data are not acceptable. Respect boundaries and stop when asked. Do not turn learning discussions into assessments of a person's intelligence or employability.

The maintainer, [arvindm1991](https://github.com/arvindm1991), may edit or remove disruptive content, close a discussion or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explained when doing so does not disclose private information. Ask the maintainer for a private channel to report sensitive conduct concerns; do not publish the details in an issue.
